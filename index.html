Slide 02 / 25 Next: The Answer in the Question →
FOUNDATIONS • VERB TENSE RADAR

The Answer is in the Question

Never freeze trying to conjugate verbs. The interviewer's auxiliary verb reveals the exact grammatical tense for your opening sentence.

DID you...?

Target: Past Simple (-ed or irregular)

Q: "Did you manage that alone?"
A: "I coordinated the creative team in Slack..."
HAVE you ever...?

Target: Present Perfect (Have + Past Part.)

Q: "Have you worked with overseas clients?"
A: "I have managed website and marketing for a London clinic..."
HOW DO you...?

Target: Present Simple (Habits & Systems)

Q: "How do you check visual assets?"
A: "I review hierarchy, typography, and brief alignment..."
WOULD you be...?

Target: Conditional (I would / I'm ready to)

Q: "Would you work around worship broadcasts?"
A: "I would be fully available on call for Sunday needs..."

FOUNDATIONS • GRAMMAR RADAR

Express Verb Tenses Radar

Sincroniza tu respuesta con la intención exacta de la pregunta para sonar seguro y evitar errores de conjugación.

1. Present Simple

RUTINAS, SISTEMAS Y HÁBITOS

Trigger: "How do you...", "What does your day look like?"

Fórmula: I + Verbo Base
• "Every morning, I review Slack and distribute tasks."
• "Before publishing, I check typography and hierarchy."
2. Present Perfect

TRAYECTORIA Y HERRAMIENTAS

Trigger: "Have you worked with...", "What tools have you used?"

Fórmula: I have + Participio (-ed)
• "I have managed web updates for a London clinic."
• "I have used Claude and Runway to accelerate ideation."
3. Past Simple

ANÉCDOTAS PUNTUALES (CRISIS)

Trigger: "Tell me about a time you...", "What happened when...?"

Fórmula: I + Verbo en Pasado
• "When an asset failed, I isolated the template and fixed it."
• "Early on, I learned to work with staged reviews."
4. Conditional "Would"

DISPONIBILIDAD Y LÍMITES

Trigger: "Would you work...", "What would you do if...?"

Fórmula: I would / My step would be
• "I would be fully available on call for Sunday worship."
• "If three tasks landed, I would prioritize broadcast assets."

FOUNDATIONS • FLUENCY ACCELERATORS

Time-Buyers: Eradicate "Um... Eh..."

Senior professionals never fill silence with vocal hesitations. Use structured verbal bridges to buy 3 to 5 seconds of thinking time.

1. The Analytical Bridge

When organizing complex workflows:

• "That's a very practical question. The way I organize that is..."

• "There are two main phases in my daily review process..."
2. The Experience Anchor

When grounding your answer in past work:

• "In my day-to-day role leading design at Owlbox..."

• "That actually comes up regularly with our London clients at wewant studio..."
3. The Clarification Pivot

When a prompt is too open-ended:

• "Just to clarify the focus: are we looking at live sermon clip turnarounds or weekly campaign concept development?"
Slide 05 / 25 Next: Circumlocution Protocol →
FOUNDATIONS • EMERGENCY TOOLKIT

The Dynamic Circumlocution Protocol

Never panic when a technical word slips your mind. Use the Action + Concrete Outcome formula.

The Emergency Rescue Matrix

Replace missing technical terms with simple, descriptive actions:

• Forgot "Repurposing"? → "Taking long recordings and cutting them into vertical clips."
• Forgot "Copywriting / Scripting"? → "Drafting the scripts and text for video and posts."
• Forgot "Visual Hierarchy"? → "Making sure the eye reads the most important text first."
• Forgot "Prompt Engineering"? → "Writing specific instructions for tools like Claude or Runway."
• Forgot "On Call"? → "Being available to jump on an urgent fix if anything happens."
The 3-Second Composure Reset

If your train of thought drifts, never apologize profusely. Use an executive reset:

Executive Reset Phrases
"Let me rephrase that to be completely clear..." or "The bottom line is..."

Recruiters judge emotional control. A composed pause followed by a clean summary projects leadership maturity.

FOUNDATIONS • ACTIVE STEERING

The Conversation Loop (A-C-E)

Transform the interview from a one-sided interrogation into an executive business dialogue.

A - Answer (30-45s)

Lead with the conclusion, followed by 1 or 2 concrete examples. Avoid lengthy preludes.

C - Connect (10s)

Bridge the experience directly to Harvest Church, Bishop Foreman, or the agency's daily flow.

E - Engage (The Loop Question)

Pass the ball back with an open-ended operational question that confirms your alignment.

Agustín's Real Strategic Questions (Use to Close Answers)
1. "Is this a brand-new role, or what key milestones are you hoping the incoming manager will improve?"
2. "Do you already have a defined visual strategy separating Harvest Church from Bishop Foreman's personal brand?"
3. "How does the current content review and approval flow operate between the media team and leadership?"
